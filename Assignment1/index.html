The Noisy House - Project Documentation

The Noisy House is a musical game that helps toddlers explore and mix music using familiar everyday objects. By clicking, hovering, and moving their mouse, children can experiment with sounds and create their own music without needing to understand musical rules or instructions. This the aim of this document ia to highlight design process, main interaction of the noisy house project

Chosen Design Scenario

Scenario 3: Musical Toy for Toddlers

The aim of this scenario is to design a musical toy that young children, who have minimal language skills. The goal of this design is to be easy to understand and interact with. The design should encourage them to experiment with music and learn through play.

How The Noisy House Responds to the Design Scenario ?

The Noisy House responds to this scenario by making the visual and interaction simple. I decided to use game to help children learn about music by interacting with sound in the form of familiar household object instead of traditional music making tools, that have complicated instructions and rules. It might be overwhelming for toddlers

The interface mainly uses visual interaction, so it is easy for children to understand how to play without relying on language skills It also encourages toddlers to experiment by mixing multiple sound together and create their own music

The Noisy House

When user open the link, They will see a pastel, colourful ilustration, a picture of room they fimilar with such as, kitchen and bathroom. Each room contains familiar household objects that children can interact with to discover sounds and create their own musical combinations.

Core Interactive

1. Click and Hover

This game use simple interaction like mouse clicking only, which make it accesible for toddlers

Click

- Clicking an object to start or stoop the sound loop

- Clicking multipl object at once to make their own musical compositions

Hover

- Hovering the mouse over an object to lister to sound preview

- Children can explore new sound before deciding which sound they want to include in their music

2. Extended Technique: Mouse Movement

Move Mouse Up: lounder

Move Mouse Down: quieter

To make the interaction more intersting and fun. I add this extended technique, where when player can move mouse around the canvas to play with volume. When the cursor is moving up, the sound becomes louder and when it is moving down, the sound becomes quieter. The idea of this interaction is to minimize button or slider that toddler might still not understand how it work and they can have fun with volume of the sound as well. I understand having sound up and down can be confusing, that is why I added volume icon at the right bottom of the page, so user receive the visual feedback and be less confuse why suddenly the sound is quieter.

Visual Style and Colour

In this project I use pastel colour mainly because the target audience is toddlers so it should be colourful and soft, so it is not overwhelming but still interesting to them Main colours is Blue and orange. I use blue in bathroom scene which repate to water and Orange in kitchen scene which relate to fire in the kitchen. The drawing style also simple and clean ilustration inspire by children book make it childrens friendly. I designed button(object) like a sticker because young children love sticker and that is why the background have paper texture.
